Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What we collect

When an account is connected through the platform's official login, Social-Publisher receives:

  • an access token issued by the platform;
  • basic account information: account ID, display name, avatar, username;
  • where permitted, statistics of the account's own content: views, likes, comments, shares and follower counts.

How we use it

Only to upload and schedule the account owner's videos, and to show the owner performance reports of their own content. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Storage & security

Tokens and data are stored privately on the owner's own devices and private cloud storage, accessible only to the owner.

Retention & deletion

Data is kept only while an account stays connected. To revoke access or request deletion, disconnect the app in the platform's settings or email us; data is deleted within 30 days.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13.
